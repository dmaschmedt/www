Beth Weber

Beth joined Maschmedt & Associates in 2005 as a Sales Administrator, beginning her career in a unique role embedded within one of the company’s Manufacturing partners in Minnesota. She provided sales support to the company’s Sales Associates and their key accounts, gaining valuable insight into both the sales process and the needs of Maschmedt’s Customers and Manufacturers.

In 2007, Beth relocated with Maschmedt & Associates to the company’s Anaheim, California office. This move expanded her experience and provided the opportunity to work closely with all of Maschmedt’s Manufacturing partners while continuing to support the Sales Team and their Customers.

Over the years, Beth has developed strong, lasting relationships with Sales Associates, Manufacturers, and Customers. Today, as Director of Administration, she plays an integral role in supporting the company’s day-to-day operations and sales efforts from the Shawnee, Kansas office.
